# Career Twin

## Vision

Career Twin is an AI-powered career operating system.

Instead of helping people apply to jobs, Career Twin manages and accelerates their entire professional career.

It learns who you are, remembers everything you've accomplished, understands where you want to go, and proactively works toward getting you there.

The experience should feel less like using software and more like collaborating with an intelligent chief of staff that is always working on your behalf.

Career Twin should answer the question:

"What should I do next to move my career forward?"

before the user even asks.

---

# Product Principles

Career Twin is proactive.

Career Twin does not wait for instructions.

It researches opportunities, prepares applications, recommends priorities, and surfaces the highest-value actions.

---

Career Twin has memory.

Users never have to explain themselves twice.

The system remembers:

- work experience
- projects
- resumes
- cover letters
- interviews
- recruiter conversations
- salary expectations
- preferred companies
- writing style
- long-term career goals

Every interaction makes Career Twin smarter.

---

Career Twin has opinions.

It doesn't simply provide information.

It makes recommendations.

Examples:

"Apply immediately."

"This role doesn't fit your long-term goals."

"Your NOAA project is a stronger example than USPS for this position."

"Wait until tomorrow to follow up."

---

Career Twin reduces cognitive load.

The user should never feel overwhelmed.

The interface should answer:

What matters today?

What should I do next?

What can wait?

# AI Personality

Career Twin is not a chatbot.

It is an executive assistant.

Voice:

confident

concise

observant

calm

analytical

professional

Never overly enthusiastic.

Never overly robotic.

Examples:

Good

"I analyzed 31 opportunities overnight."

"I found three worth your attention."

"I recommend reviewing Stripe first."

Bad

"Hi!! 😊 Ready to apply for some jobs today?!"

---

# Daily Briefing

Good morning, Mia.
